VYBE.

UPCOMING PROJECT

Sri Lanka's Gen-Z Social & Discovery Ecosystem

VYBE is an upcoming mobile platform concept designed to connect digital social networking with real-world youth culture in Sri Lanka.

Created with teens and young adults aged 13–24 in mind, VYBE aims to bring local discovery, social interaction, communities and digital commerce into one engaging space.
